# Arithmetic recovery v5

The stage-6 model had correct multiplication partial products but incorrect sums, borrowing errors and incorrect quotients despite decreasing training loss. The original addition operands stopped below 10,000 although 2,719/7,500 multiplication tasks required larger partial-product sums. Existing remediation repeated the original targets.

A separate immutable derivative at `G:/ctfn-text/data/v3_math_recovery_v5` preserves the original train/validation/test records and adds 32,000 training and 1,024 validation records. Each of four skills has 4,000 fresh training operand families and 128 held-out families, with paired direct and place-value scaffold representations. Direct/scaffold variants stay in the same split. Generated families exclude original training, validation, test and quarantined tasks, including commutative add/multiply variants. No sealed test scores were inspected. All new gold targets passed the procedure scorer; actual tokenizer maximum is 333/2048. Intermediate-equation tests independently verify 400 randomized scaffold traces.

The recovery queue is partial-product addition, subtraction, division, multiplication. Sampling uses 80% focused fresh examples and 20% other current/prior skills. Direct/scaffold forms and meaningful procedural subcases are balanced. Routine active and retention panels run every round; full focused validation includes original and new held-out examples and runs every five recovery rounds, followed by the next round after a pass. Two consecutive full focused passes advance the queue. A skill still failing after 240 recovery rounds pauses for inspection. Loss is diagnostic and does not gate recovery.

After the queue passes, normal stage mixture and full mastery/retention promotion resume, with a fresh normal budget and automatic validation enabled for this stage. Later-stage settings remain unchanged. Current checkpoint weights, optimizer and RNG were retained, with a separate `before_arithmetic_recovery_round_4012.specialist` backup. The partial round restarts because its sampling distribution changed. Completed stages are retained. The adoption script binds the checkpoint to the new manifest; the original dataset is unchanged.

Validation: controller and scaffold tests, existing curriculum regression tests, and a disposable mixed direct/scaffold CUDA optimizer update with finite gradients. The CUDA smoke does not save its modified weights. Effectiveness must still be measured by recovery validation; implementation checks do not establish improved model accuracy.
