# V3.1 fresh maths training

V3.1 is a separate checkout at C:/CFTN/V3.1 on branch codex/v3.1. V3.0 was safely paused at round 5591 and its final checkpoint was copied to G:/ctfn-text/artifacts/v3_curriculum_v4/math/final_before_v3_1.specialist. No prior weights or optimizer state are imported into V3.1.

The maths tower grows from 7,172,865 to 9,323,361 total parameters (+29.98%). It retains eight layers, eight heads and a 2,048-byte context; hidden width is 288 and feed-forward width is 1,232. Fresh matrix weights use normal initialization with standard deviation 0.02, seed 3109307. Layer norms retain unit scale. The auxiliary answer classifier remains frozen. The new model has 9,027,136 trainable parameters.

The main dataset G:/ctfn-text/data/v3_1_main includes all original and arithmetic-recovery training examples. All remediation files were merged by unique criterion/semantic identity; their records were already included. The builder adds place-value worked targets to eligible arithmetic at its original stage, so the format and primitive operations are taught before multi-digit composition. Original direct targets are preserved. Newly added scaffold variants remain in the same split as their source tasks; held-out families are excluded from new training variants. All gold targets and actual-tokenizer lengths were checked. Totals: 272,904 train, 9,863 validation, 8,892 sealed test; 48,131 additional training scaffolds. Maximum sequence length: 641/2,048. These are selected procedural mathematics, not comprehensive graduate mathematics.

The run starts fresh at stage 1 using config/local_curriculum_v31.json. Artifacts reside at G:/ctfn-text/artifacts/v3_1. Normal sampling is 75% active and 25% prior-stage replay where applicable. SIGReg is 0.0003 and learning rate 0.00001. The loss threshold is 0.003, with no pre-threshold round limit. Validation then stays enabled for the stage, with normal/recovery gates and two consecutive full passes for promotion. Manual diagnostic checks remain available. The existing String queue remains after Maths acceptance; its architecture and dataset are unchanged.

Validation includes existing curriculum/recovery tests and a disposable eight-update RTX 4070 check confirming finite gradients and decreased training loss. Smoke-test weights are not saved to the initial training checkpoint. A larger model and improved curriculum are hypotheses to test, not evidence of improved held-out accuracy.

## Strict foundation gates

For stages 1-5, every active and retained criterion and decision/procedural stratum must score 100% answers, traces and format. This applies to routine, full promotion, manual diagnostics and the final test for these stages. Promotion still requires two consecutive full passes. Stages 6 onward retain the existing thresholds. Loss remains a scheduling threshold, not proof of mastery. The policy is checkpoint-bound; changing it resets pass streaks and preserves weights, optimizer, RNG, round counters and cursor with a backup.

V3.0 promotion audit: stage 1 active answers/traces 97.10% (69 questions); stage 2 answers 99.16%, traces 99.02%, retention answers 98.92%; stage 3 active 97.39%, retention 98.27%; stage 4 active 100% (21 questions), retention 97.71%; stage 5 active 100% (16 questions), retention answers 97.47%, traces 97.37%. Format scores were 100%. Stage 1 evidence is from the inherited v3 curriculum; stages 2-5 from v4. The historical policy allowed these results. They establish imperfect foundations, but do not establish that those imperfections caused every later failure.
